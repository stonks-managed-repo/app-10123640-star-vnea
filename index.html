About Star

The plain facts

Star — ticker $STAR — is a token on the TON blockchain. It was launched on Stonks, the TON launch platform at app.stonks.cash, which means the token was put into circulation openly, on-chain, where anyone with a TON wallet can find it and trade it.

There is deliberately little more to say than that. Star is not dressed up with promises it cannot keep. What the token is — its name, its ticker, the chain it lives on and the place it was launched — is exactly what you see on this page, and it is all verifiable on-chain.

“One name. One ticker. One chain. Everything else is up to the people who hold it.”

The life of Star happens in two places. The market lives on Stonks, where $STAR is bought and sold against TON. The conversation lives in the official Telegram community, where holders gather, ask questions and follow along. If you want to understand Star, those are the two doors — and both are open.

Star belongs to the TON ecosystem: fast, low-fee, and native to Telegram, the network where its community already lives. That is the whole pitch, and it is enough.

How to buy $STAR

  2. 2

    Fund it with TON or GRAM

    Send TON (or GRAM) to your new wallet from an exchange or another wallet. Keep a little extra TON on hand — it covers the network fee for the swap.

  3. 3

    Open app.stonks.cash, connect your wallet, search for Star ($STAR), enter the amount and confirm the transaction in your wallet.
